SAIL_VWAP_ST

Trade SAIL (Steel Authority of India) options off its 5-minute chart. Wait for a candle to close across VWAP, then wait for a second candle to close past that first candle's high or low. Buy only if SuperTrend points the same way. The SuperTrend line is the stop.

1. In one minute

  • Watch SAIL's 5-minute candles and its VWAP line. Only closed candles count.
  • Step 1 — cross. A candle closes above VWAP after the one before it closed at or below. That is the cross candle. Note its high.
  • Step 2 — breakout. Within the next 3 candles, one closes above the cross candle's high. Every close in between must stay above VWAP.
  • Step 3 — SuperTrend agrees. SuperTrend (10, 2) is green. Then buy the ATM SAIL CE at the next tick. If it is red, the setup is skipped.
  • PE is the mirror: close below VWAP, then a close below the cross candle's low, SuperTrend red.
  • The stop is the SuperTrend line. It only moves your way. The trade ends when a candle closes through it, or SuperTrend changes colour.
  • On top of that, the repo-wide profit lock, breakeven stop and a 25% option-price stop watch every tick. Everything is closed at 15:15.
  • New trades 09:30–14:30. One trade a day. ₹5,000 daily loss limit. One lot = 4,700 shares.

2. Words you'll see

WordPlain meaning
CandleOne 5-minute bar: open, high, low, close. Times are the candle's start, as on Kite: the 10:05 candle runs 10:05–10:10.
VWAPThe day's average price, weighted by volume. Each candle adds (high + low + close) ÷ 3 × its volume. It restarts every morning — the same as Kite's “VWAP hlc3, Session”.
Cross candleThe candle that closes on the other side of VWAP from the candle before it.
Breakout candleThe first later candle (within 3) that closes past the cross candle's high (CE) or low (PE). It is the signal.
SuperTrend (10, 2)A line set 2 × the average candle size (last 10 candles) away from price. Green and under price = up. Red and over price = down. It is also the stop.
CE / PEA CE (call) gains when SAIL rises. A PE (put) gains when SAIL falls. This strategy only buys.
ATM strikeThe strike nearest SAIL's price on the ₹2.50 grid. SAIL at 162.64 → the 162.50 strike.
Monthly expirySAIL is a stock, so it has no weekly options. The bot always uses the current month's contract.
Lot4,700 SAIL shares. Every ₹1 SAIL moves changes one lot's option by roughly ₹2,350, if the option moves half as much as the share (delta 0.5).
Square-offClosing any open trade at 15:15 so nothing is held overnight.

3. How a trade starts

Every step is read on a closed candle. A wick does not count.

Buy a CE (call)
1
Cross. A candle closes above VWAP. The candle before it, the same day, closed at or below VWAP.
2
Breakout. One of the next 3 candles closes above the cross candle's high. Only the first such close counts.
3
Hold above VWAP. Every close from the cross candle to the breakout candle stays above VWAP. One close back at or below VWAP cancels the setup.
4
SuperTrend is green on the breakout candle (its line is under the close). If it is red, the setup is skipped — there is no line under price to use as a stop.
5
Time. The breakout candle closes between 09:30 and 14:30, no trade yet today, and the day's loss is under ₹5,000.
Real example (28 Jul 2026). The 10:00 candle closes at 160.76, under VWAP 160.80. The 10:05 candle closes at 161.40, above VWAP 160.84 — the cross. Its high is 161.45. The 10:10 candle closes at 161.20: still above VWAP (160.86) but not above 161.45, so the bot waits. The 10:15 candle closes at 162.62 — above 161.45. SuperTrend is green at 160.94. Buy the 162.50 CE at the next tick (SAIL 162.64). Stop = 160.94.
Buy a PE (put) — the mirror
  • A candle closes below VWAP after one that closed at or above it.
  • Within 3 candles, one closes below the cross candle's low, with no close back at or above VWAP in between.
  • SuperTrend is red (its line is over the close).
Real example (25 Aug 2026). The 10:15 candle closes at 183.20, below VWAP 183.70, after the 10:10 candle closed above it — the cross. Its low is 183.20. The 10:20 candle closes at 182.60 — below 183.20. SuperTrend is red at 184.27. Buy the 182.50 PE (SAIL 182.60). Stop = 184.27.
  • The cross candle is never the entry. At least one more candle must close past its high or low.
  • A setup lasts 3 candles. No breakout close in 3 candles → the setup expires.
  • A refused breakout is used up. If the first breakout close is skipped (SuperTrend, time), a later close past the same level does not trade.
  • Fill: Paper and Live buy on the first price tick after the breakout candle closes.

4. How a trade ends

Checked when a candle closes (the strategy's own exit):

  1. SuperTrend changes colour against the trade (red on a CE, green on a PE) → sell.
  2. A candle closes beyond the stop (below it on a CE, above it on a PE) → sell.
  3. Otherwise the stop moves to the new SuperTrend value — only if that is tighter. It never moves back.
The SuperTrend stop is never checked tick by tick. A candle that dips through the line but closes back on the right side does not end the trade. The flip side: a candle that falls a long way through the line is only sold after it closes, so a loss can be bigger than the gap to the line.

Checked on every tick (shared by every strategy in this app, all on the option price):

ExitRuleExample: option bought at ₹5.00
Option-price stopSell if the premium falls 25% below the buy price (SAIL_VWAP_ST_OPT_STOP_PCT).Sell at ₹3.75 or lower.
Breakeven stopOnce the premium has been up 5%, it may not be sold below the buy price (BREAKEVEN_*).Arms at ₹5.25. Then a fall back to ₹5.00 sells.
Profit lockOnce the premium has been up 8%, at least +5% is kept, and the floor follows new highs (PROFIT_LOCK_*).Arms at ₹5.40. The floor starts at ₹5.25.
Square-off15:15 → anything still open is sold.—

There is no fixed target and no re-entry after a stop. One trade a day means a stopped trade is the day's only trade.

6. Why SuperTrend 10,2 and not 10,1

The choice was left to us. On SAIL's 5-minute chart a (10, 1) line sits too close to price to be a stop.

Measured on every candle (60 days)Typical gap to the close1 candle in 10 is closer than
SuperTrend (10, 1)₹0.40₹0.13
SuperTrend (10, 2)₹0.73₹0.28
For scale: one candle's high-to-low₹0.35—

A (10, 1) line is about one normal candle away from price, so ordinary wiggle would flip it. (10, 2) gives the trade almost twice the room. Both numbers can be changed in Settings.

7. Real chart examples

How to read the charts. These are real SAIL days, run candle by candle through the real engine with default settings, the way Paper does: 2 earlier days loaded first, decide on the close, fill at the next candle's open. Blue line = VWAP. Green/red line = SuperTrend (also the stop). Yellow dots = the cross candle and the breakout candle. The candles are Yahoo Finance's SAIL.NS data. Its volume can differ slightly from NSE's, so VWAP may differ by a few paise from Kite. Yahoo also reports zero volume for the 09:15 candle, so here VWAP starts at 09:20 — early-morning VWAP can differ from Kite by more than a few paise. Results are in SAIL rupees per share. Option prices are not shown, so the tick-level option exits (profit lock, breakeven, 25% stop) are not drawn.

A winner (CE)

What you see: 28 Jul 2026. The 10:05 candle closes at 161.40, above VWAP 160.84, after the 10:00 candle closed at 160.76 under VWAP 160.80 — the cross, high 161.45. The 10:10 candle closes at 161.20, not above 161.45: wait. The 10:15 candle closes at 162.62, SuperTrend green at 160.94 → BUY CE at 162.64 (10:20 open), 1.70 above the stop. The green line climbs under price; by 12:25 the stop is 165.90. The 13:10 candle dips to 165.89, one paise under the stop, but closes at 166.08 — no exit. The 13:15 candle closes at 165.89, under 165.90, and SuperTrend turns red → EXIT at 165.89 (13:20 open), +3.25 per share. Why it matters: with no target, the trail is what lets a trend run — and a close-only stop rides out a wick.

A loser (PE), after a skip and a whipsaw

What you see: 25 Aug 2026. The 09:30 candle closes at 184.67, below VWAP 184.80 (cross, low 184.50). The 09:35 candle closes at 183.40, below 184.50 — a PE breakout — but SuperTrend is still green at 182.91, so it is skipped. SuperTrend turns red on the next candle and SAIL falls to 181.71 by 09:45; the skip cost this move. From 10:00 to 10:15, four candles in a row close on alternate sides of VWAP (183.86, 183.06, 183.78, 183.20); each close cancels the setup the one before started. The 10:15 cross holds: the 10:20 candle closes at 182.60, below the cross low 183.20, SuperTrend red at 184.27 → BUY PE at 182.60 (10:25 open). SAIL turns up. The 10:45 candle closes at 184.59, above the stop 184.27, and SuperTrend turns green → EXIT at 184.51 (10:50 open), −1.91 per share. A CE breakout at 11:20 (close 185.25) is ignored: one trade a day. Why it matters: a normal loss is about the gap from entry to the line.

No trade: SuperTrend disagreed three times

What you see: 6 Aug 2026. Three PE setups, three skips. 1: the 09:40 candle closes at 178.60, below VWAP 178.67 (low 178.35); the 09:45 candle closes at 178.29, below 178.35 — but SuperTrend is green at 177.42. 2: the 12:05 candle closes at 177.68, below VWAP 178.02 (low 177.62); the 12:10 candle closes at 177.61, one paise below — SuperTrend green at 177.04. 3: the 14:05 candle closes at 177.69, below VWAP 177.92 (low 177.66); the 14:10 candle closes at 177.75 (not past 177.66, wait); the 14:15 candle closes at 177.50 — SuperTrend green at 177.25. Each time the line is under price, so there is no stop above it, and the bot does nothing. After skip 1, SuperTrend turned red on the 10:00 candle and SAIL fell to 176.52 by 11:15. Why it matters: the SuperTrend rule removes trades against the trend, including some that would have worked. It is one of the 13 no-trade days among the 58.

8. What 58 days of SAIL data say

How this was measured: the engine (default settings) was walked candle by candle over 60 days of Yahoo SAIL.NS 5-minute candles (24 Jul – 19 Oct 2026). The first 2 days were warm-up, so 58 days (28 Jul – 19 Oct) could trade. Decide on the close, fill at the next open, trail on each close, square off at 15:15. Results are rupees per SAIL share before any costs. Only the SuperTrend stop and the 15:15 square-off were applied: the profit lock, breakeven stop, 25% option stop and ₹5,000 loss limit work on option prices, which this data does not have. This is a small sample. Treat it as a check that the engine works, not as proof of an edge.

10. What's still missing

  • It has never traded. No paper session, no live order. Every number above is from old candles.
  • No option prices were tested. The 60-day check is in SAIL rupees per share. The backtest's rupee figures are simulated: an option price built from an assumed starting premium, delta 0.5 and a fixed daily time decay — not real SAIL option quotes.
  • The tick-level exits were not tested. Profit lock, breakeven and the 25% option stop need real option ticks. They will change the results above, most likely by cutting winners short.
  • The stop can cost most of a day's limit. The middle first stop is ₹1.41 away (1 in 10 over ₹2.02); at delta 0.5 on 4,700 shares that is about ₹3,300–₹4,750 in one trade. SAIL_VWAP_ST_ST_MULT is the lever: smaller = tighter stop but more noise exits.
  • The stop only checks candle closes. 9 of 29 losers lost more than their starting stop distance.
  • Slow to learn. One trade a day, and none on about 1 day in 5. At that pace 30 trades take about 39 sessions — roughly eight weeks.
  • Small, recent sample. 58 days of one stock: SAIL ran from about ₹160 to ₹200 and back to about ₹169. The data is Yahoo's, not Fyers', so VWAP (and some crosses) can differ from what the bot sees live.
  • SAIL options trade far less than NIFTY's. The bid-ask spread is a real cost on every trade; nothing above charges for it.
  • Next step: paper-trade clean sessions on the defaults, run Live in dry-run next to Paper and check its logged orders match Paper's trades, and only then think about live.
