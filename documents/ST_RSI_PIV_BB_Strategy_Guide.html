ST_RSI_PIV_BB

Four common chart tools must all say the same thing on one closed candle: SuperTrend, RSI, yesterday's pivot level and the Bollinger Band. When all four agree, the bot buys an at-the-money option. When even one disagrees, it does nothing. The SuperTrend line is then the only stop, and it is checked only when a candle closes.

1. In one minute

  • Watch NIFTY 50 candles. Each time a candle closes, tick four boxes.
  • Buy a CE (call) when all four are true: SuperTrend is green, RSI is above 70, the close is above yesterday's R1, and the close is above the upper Bollinger Band.
  • Buy a PE (put) on the mirror: SuperTrend red, RSI below 30, close below yesterday's S1, close below the lower band.
  • Three out of four is not a trade. There is no waiting candle: the bot buys right at that candle's close.
  • The stop is the SuperTrend line. It only moves in your favour. The trade ends when a candle closes on the wrong side of it (or SuperTrend changes colour).
  • New trades only from 09:30 to 15:00. Up to 3 trades a day, a ₹5,000 daily loss limit, and everything is closed at 15:15.

Where it comes from: a course slide that said “When all four agree — that's the entry” and “3 of 4 is not a trade”. The thresholds are the slide's, not tuned numbers.

Read this first. This strategy is brand new. It has never traded — no paper session and no live order. Live orders are off by default. The charts and numbers below come from running the engine over old 5-minute NIFTY candles. They are spot points, not rupees.

2. Words you'll see

WordPlain meaning
CandleOne bar on the chart: open, high, low and close for 3 or 5 minutes. Green = closed higher than it opened, red = lower. On the charts the time under a candle is when it starts; a 10:30 five-minute candle closes at 10:35.
SuperTrend (10, 2)A line built from the average candle size over the last 10 candles, set 2 times that size away from price. Green and under price = uptrend. Red and over price = downtrend. It changes colour when a candle closes through it.
RSI (14)A 0–100 score of how hard price has pushed over the last 14 candles. Above 70 = a strong push up. Below 30 = a strong push down.
Bollinger Band (20, 2)A channel around the 20-candle average, 2 “normal swings” wide each side. A close outside the band is an unusually big move.
Pivot, R1, S1Three numbers worked out before the open from yesterday's high, low and close. PP = (high + low + close) ÷ 3, R1 = 2×PP − low, S1 = 2×PP − high. They stay fixed all day.
CE / PEA CE (call) gains when NIFTY rises. A PE (put) gains when NIFTY falls. This strategy only buys, at the money.
Stop / trailThe level where the trade is closed to cap a loss. “Trailing” means it follows the trade, and here it never moves back.
Square-offClosing any open trade at 15:15 so nothing is held overnight.
R1 and S1 with real numbers. On 2025-06-13 NIFTY made high 24,754.35, low 24,473.00 and closed at 24,735.55. So for the next trading day PP = 24,654.30, R1 = 24,835.60 and S1 = 24,554.25. Those are the lines on the first chart in Section 7.

The move is bigger than normal.
  • Only the close counts. A wick above R1 that closes back below it leaves Box 3 empty.
  • Any close past R1 counts, not only the first one. If price has been above R1 since 10:00 and the other three boxes tick at 11:40, that is a trade.
  • No waiting candle. Paper and Live buy on the first price tick after the candle closes.
  • One safety check: SuperTrend must be on the protective side of the close (under it for a CE, over it for a PE). A green line normally is: in the history test this check blocked 0 candles.
  • The starting stop is the SuperTrend value on the entry candle.

4. Which chart: 3, 5 or BOTH

The setting ST_RSI_PIV_BB_ENTRY_TF picks which candles the four boxes are read on.

SettingBoxes are read onThe trade and its stop live on
3the 3-minute candle3-minute candles
5the 5-minute candle5-minute candles
BOTH (default)the 3-minute candle and the latest closed 5-minute candle — both must be 4 of 4, same side3-minute candles

With BOTH, each time a 3-minute candle closes the bot looks back for the newest 5-minute candle that has already closed. It never reads a 5-minute candle that is still forming. Both charts are built from the same price feed.

3-min candle closes at5-min candle it reads
09:45the one that closed at 09:45 (same moment)
09:48the one that closed at 09:45
09:51the one that closed at 09:50
09:54the one that closed at 09:50

These rows are the engine's own answer (_confirmIndex), not worked out by hand.

Example. At 09:48 the 3-minute candle ticks all four CE boxes. The bot then checks the 5-minute candle that closed at 09:45. If that one is also 4 of 4 for a CE, it buys. If it is only 3 of 4 (say RSI 68), it does nothing — and checks again at 09:51 against the 09:50 candle.

5. How a trade ends

  1. SuperTrend changes colour against the trade (turns red on a CE, green on a PE) on a closed candle → sell.
  2. A candle closes beyond the stop (below it for a CE, above it for a PE) → sell.
  3. Otherwise the stop moves to the new SuperTrend value — only if that is tighter. It never moves back.
  4. 15:15 → anything still open is sold.
The stop is checked only when a candle closes — never tick by tick. If a candle dips through the line but closes back on the right side, the trade stays open. The other side of that coin: if one candle falls a long way through the line, the bot only sells after that candle closes, so the loss can be much bigger than the gap between entry and the line. Section 7 shows both.

In practice rules 1 and 2 are one event. The stop is always the SuperTrend line itself (the stop in force matched the previous candle's SuperTrend on 32,183 of 32,183 candles in the history test), and a close through that line is exactly what turns SuperTrend the other colour.

There is no target, no profit booking, no move-to-breakeven, no option-price stop and no time stop.

Daily loss limit₹5,000No new trade after the day's losses reach this.
Warm-up25 candles per chartThe engine refuses to judge until it has 25 candles. The last 3 days are loaded at start so this is ready from 09:30.

Deliberately left out. No confirmation candle, no VIX filter, no OI filter, no ADX, no volume test, no EMA, no VWAP, no target, no breakeven, no premium stop and no cool-down after a loss. Adding any of these makes it a different strategy that needs its own testing.

7. Real chart examples

How to read the charts. These are real NIFTY 50 days from the cached history, run through the real engine with ST_RSI_PIV_BB_ENTRY_TF=5. They are 5-minute charts because 3-minute history was not available offline — the default setting (BOTH) trades on 3-minute candles, so live trades will look tighter. Green/red line = SuperTrend (also the stop). Purple band = Bollinger. Blue dashed = R1, pink dashed = S1. Yellow dot = the 4-of-4 candle. Lower panel = RSI with 70 and 30 marked. Results are in NIFTY spot points; option prices are not shown.

A winner (CE)

What you see: 16 Jun 2025. The 10:30 candle closes at 24,845.85: SuperTrend green, RSI 71.17, above R1 24,835.60 and above the upper band 24,835.70. All four boxes → BUY CE at 24,846.85 (the 10:35 open). The first stop is the SuperTrend at 24,788.02, 58.83 points away. The green line then climbs under price all afternoon. The 14:35 candle closes at 24,931.50, under the line (24,932.89), and SuperTrend turns red: EXIT at 24,931.40 on the 14:40 open, +84.55 points. Why it matters: with no target, the trail is what lets a trend day run.

A loser (PE)

What you see: 9 Dec 2024. The 10:05 candle closes at 24,594.50: SuperTrend red at 24,655.45, RSI 29.64, below S1 24,609.32 and below the lower band 24,602.76 → BUY PE at 24,593.95 (10:10 open), 61.50 points from the line. Price turns up instead. At 10:45 a candle spikes to 24,646.05, above the stop 24,645.37, but closes back at 24,629.20 — no exit. The 11:15 candle closes at 24,648.00, above the stop 24,645.37, and SuperTrend turns green: EXIT at 24,648.55 (11:20 open), −54.60 points. Why it matters: a normal loss is about the gap between entry and the line.

The stop waits for the close — good and bad

What you see: 4 Dec 2024. The 09:30 candle closes at 24,540.15 (above R1 24,524.80 and the band 24,516.79, RSI 75.69) → BUY CE at 24,540.20, first stop 24,479.84. The stop trails up to 24,517.21. At 10:20, 10:25 and 10:45 candles dip under it (the 10:20 low is 24,508.75) and close back above — a tick-level stop would have sold at 10:20 for about −23 points. Then the 11:55 candle opens at 24,532.05, falls to 24,366.30 and closes at 24,414.00, 103.21 points under the line. The bot can only sell after that close: EXIT at 24,416.35 (12:00 open), −123.85 points — 2.1 times the starting stop distance of 60.36. Why it matters: the close-only stop ignores wicks, but one big candle can cost far more than the line suggests.

3 of 4 — no trade

What you see: 24 Oct 2024. From the 09:30 candle to the 09:45 candle, 4 candles in a row have SuperTrend red, RSI under 30 (down to 19.58) and a close under the lower band. But every close stays above S1 24,341.32 (closes from 24,395.65 down to 24,354.60). The 09:40 candle's low touches 24,341.20, just 0.12 under S1 — a wick, not a close. No candle closes below S1 all day, so there is no trade. Why it matters: “3 of 4 is not a trade” — even when the three are very strong.

8. What 8½ years of 5-min data say

How this was measured: the engine (ENTRY_TF=5) was walked candle by candle over cached NIFTY 5-minute candles from 2018-01-01 to 2026-07-31 (103 months, 2,111 sessions), exactly as Paper would: decide on the close, fill at the next open, trail on each close, square off at 15:15, at most 3 trades a day. Results are NIFTY spot points before any costs (no slippage, brokerage or taxes) and not rupees — an option moves only part of each spot point. The ₹5,000 daily loss limit could not be applied in points, so it was left out. The default BOTH setting needs 3-minute history and was not tested.

So on 5-minute candles, 4 of 4 is not rare: 60.3% of sessions had a trade. When a candle got 3 of 4, the Bollinger box was the one missing most often. BOTH needs a 3-minute and a 5-minute candle to agree at the same moment, so it can only trade less often than the 3-minute setting on its own — how often is not measured yet.

In plain words: on 5-minute history fewer than half the trades won (46.3%), but winners were bigger than losers, leaving about +7.44 points a trade before costs. That edge is small. Treat this as a sanity check that the engine works on real data, not as proof that the strategy makes money.

10. What's still missing

  • It has never traded. No paper session, no live order. Every number above is from old candles.
  • The default chart was not tested. BOTH (and 3) need 3-minute candles, and only 5-minute history was available offline. 3-minute candles are smaller, so stops will be tighter and signals may come more or less often.
  • Paper and Live only. This strategy has no Backtest or Replay page. The history numbers here come from a one-off offline check of old candles, in spot points only — no rupees.
  • The stop only checks candle closes. A fast move inside one candle can lose far more than the line suggests — 182 of 798 losers in the history test lost more than their starting stop distance, the worst trade lost 304 points.
  • 4 of 4 is common on 5-minute candles (about 14.4 trades a month), and the edge before costs is small (+7.44 points a trade).
  • Next step: collect clean paper sessions on the default setting, run Live in dry-run next to Paper and check its logged orders match Paper's trades, and only then think about live.
